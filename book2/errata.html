A truthful book owes its readers this page. This is the page the book's last chapter promised: corrections first, news beside them, because both come from the same place — somebody checked. Mistakes found after publication get listed here, with the correction, dated, in the order they were reported.

Corrections

Nothing has been reported yet. When something is — a wrong number in the worked example, a garbled character in a quotation, a calendar edge the calculator gets wrong — it will appear here, plainly stated, and stay here. A book that hides its corrections is asking you to trust it twice.

The book expects its first corrections to come from calendar edges — the chapter-three seams: births straddling Lìchūn, births in the night rat-hour, and lunar-calendar dates remembered as birthdays. The calculator takes western (Gregorian) dates only; if the date you know is a lunar date, chapter three explains the conversion and its traps.

News

Nothing to announce yet. The next volume of the series will be announced here when it survives the same checking this one did. Books of this kind are announced too early and read too late; the honest schedule is the one the pages set.

How to report something

Write to the publisher's customer questions page for this book, or use the contact address printed in the book's appendix. Reporters of genuine errors are thanked by name on this page, if they want to be.
