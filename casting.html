The four-question checklist, applied to this tool:
Does it make you write the question first? — Yes, it refused to start until you did.
Does it record the date? — Yes, above, with the question.
Does it show the moving lines as they fall, without rewriting them into a verdict? — Yes; the coin values are printed and nothing is translated into advice.
Does it cite what text the judgment comes from? — Yes, in the note below the result.

This tool describes a cultural practice and a framework for thinking. It offers no medical, financial, or legal advice, and nothing on it is presented as knowledge of the future.
