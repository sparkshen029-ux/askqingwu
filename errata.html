A truthful book owes its readers this page. Mistakes found after printing get listed here, with the correction, dated, in the order they were reported.

Nothing has been reported yet. When something is — a wrong number in an example, a garbled character in a quotation, a claim that doesn't hold — it will appear here, plainly stated, and stay here. A book that hides its corrections is asking you to trust it twice.

Found something? Readers who write in about a genuine error get thanked by name in the next printing, if they want to be.
