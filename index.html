A book that teaches you how to read, not what will happen

Old Master Qing Wu has spent forty years teaching the Changes in market towns and teahouses. In this book he walks you through the whole apparatus — the two kinds of line, the sixty-four figures, the rules of position and timing, the vocabulary of outcomes — until it stops being a fortune-telling toy and becomes what it was built to be: a clear-eyed tool for looking at a decision you already have to make.

Every Chinese quotation is printed on the page with a plain-English explanation, so you can check every single thing he tells you. Where he cannot verify something, he says so. You will not be told what will happen to you. You will learn something more useful: how to ask.

Available as a Kindle ebook from Amazon. The ebook carries the Chinese text, the pinyin, the figures, and three appendices — the sixty-four hexagrams at a glance, the terms used in the book, and full sources.

About Old Master Qing Wu

Old Master Qing Wu (青乌) has spent forty years teaching the Changes — the book Western readers call the I Ching — in market towns, teahouses, and one Tuesday-night class that has outlived three teapots. He does not tell fortunes and does not want to. He teaches people how to ask a question clearly, how to read an answer honestly, and how to put the decision back where it belongs. This is his first book for readers outside the tradition.
